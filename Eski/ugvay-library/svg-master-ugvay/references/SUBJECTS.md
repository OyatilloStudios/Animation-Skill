# Subject recipes & accuracy notes

## Biology
- Animal cell: nucleus (DNA, nucleolus → ribosome synthesis), mitochondria (ATP, cellular respiration), rough ER (ribosomes, protein synthesis), Golgi (packaging), lysosome (digestion), membrane (selective permeability). Plant cell adds wall (cellulose), chloroplast (photosynthesis), large central vacuole. Plants have no lysosome-centred digestion focus and no centrioles.
- Mitosis order: (interphase) → prophase → metaphase → anaphase → telophase → cytokinesis. Result: 2 identical diploid cells. Meiosis: 4 haploid cells, crossing over in prophase I.
- DNA: A–T (2 H-bonds), G–C (3 H-bonds), antiparallel, ~10.5 bp per turn. RNA uses U instead of T.
- Photosynthesis: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O →(light, chlorophyll) C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂. Respiration is the reverse, produces ~30–32 ATP.
- Enzyme: active site, substrate specificity (lock-and-key / induced fit), not consumed, denatured by heat/pH.
- Recipe: `cell` labels → camera zoom on part → `flow` of the process → quiz.

## Medicine / physiology
- Heart: deoxygenated blood → vena cava → right atrium → tricuspid → right ventricle → pulmonary artery → lungs → pulmonary veins → left atrium → mitral → left ventricle → aorta. Left ventricle wall is thickest. Resting rate 60–100 bpm (≈70).
- Neuron: dendrites → soma → axon (myelin, nodes of Ranvier, saltatory conduction) → terminals → synapse (neurotransmitter). Resting potential ≈ −70 mV, action potential peak ≈ +30…+40 mV; Na⁺ in, then K⁺ out.
- Blood: RBC (hemoglobin, O₂), WBC (immunity), platelets (clotting). Normal BP ≈ 120/80 mmHg.
- Always add "maslahat uchun shifokorga murojaat qiling" for any treatment topic; never give dosages.
- Recipe: `heart`/`neuron`/`vessel` preset + `flow` of the pathway + `number` for key values.

## Chemistry
- Balance every equation (atoms conserved). Use `reaction` (`left`, `right` with coefficients) or `formula` with `chem:true`.
- Bohr shells: 2, 8, 8, 18 (simplified; for Z ≤ 20 use 2-8-8-2 rule). Water bond angle 104.5°, CO₂ linear, CH₄ tetrahedral 109.5°, NH₃ pyramidal 107°.
- Exothermic: combustion (`fire`, `explosion`), neutralisation. Show conditions (t°, catalyst, pressure) with `condition`.
- Recipe: `atom` → `molecule` labels → `reaction` → `chart` (energy diagram via `fns`) → quiz.

## Physics
- Use the simulations — they integrate the real equations; give realistic SI values (g = 9.8 m/s²).
- Pendulum period T = 2π√(L/g) (small angles). Spring T = 2π√(m/k). Projectile range L = v₀² sin2α / g, max at 45° (no drag). Kepler 2: equal areas in equal times; 3: T² ∝ a³. Snell: n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂; TIR when θ > arcsin(n₂/n₁). Ohm: I = U/R, P = UI. Lens: 1/f = 1/d + 1/d′. Waves: v = λf.
- Recipe: phenomenon with effect → simulation (left2) + formula (right2) → change one parameter in the next scene (e.g. angle 30° vs 60°) → quiz.

## Mathematics
- `chart` for functions (`fns`, `shade` for integrals, `mark` for roots/extrema), `formula` for derivations step by step (one formula item per step with `at:'+2'`), `shape`/`svg` for geometry (stage px), `arrow` for vectors.

## History & social sciences
- Dates must be exact; if disputed write "taxminan" / "~". Amir Temur: 1336 (Kesh) – 1405 (O‘tror), ruled from 1370, Ankara battle 1402. Alisher Navoiy: 1441–1501. Mirzo Ulug‘bek: 1394–1449, observatory 1420s. Ibn Sino: 980–1037, "Tib qonunlari". Al-Xorazmiy: c. 780–850, algebra. Uzbekistan independence: 1 September 1991 (declared 31 Aug 1991).
- Recipe: `timeline` (4–6 events) → `mindmap` (causes / consequences) → `compare` (before / after) → quiz.

## Geography
- Use `image` of a map with labels, `flow` for water cycle (evaporation → condensation → precipitation → collection, `cycle:true`), `rain`/`water`/`rays` effects.

## Economics
- Demand slopes down, supply up; equilibrium at intersection (`mark`). Shifts: draw old curve `dashed`, new curve solid. Inflation, GDP → `barchart`/`number`. Budget shares → `pie` (sum = 100).
